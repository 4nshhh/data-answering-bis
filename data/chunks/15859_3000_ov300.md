## 3 TERMINOLOGY

For the purpose of this standard following definitions shall apply.



### 3.1 Buffing Machine

The buffing machine separates hydrolyzed linters from cotton seed by mechanical friction in the buffer reel. The cotton seed is separated from the linter in the buffing machine and linters are dropped down through perforated buffer reels. Thus separation of cotton seeds and linters takes place in buffing machine.


### 3.2 Anhydrous HCl (AHCl) Treated Seed

The cotton seeds are treated with AHCl gas. The reaction of AHCl and exothermic heat hydrolyzes fine linters and disintegrates and becomes loose. Such cotton seeds are called as AHCl gas treated seeds.


### 3.3 Delinted Seed

The clear brown/black colour cotton seeds received after the separation of linters in buffing machine. These seeds are bold, good seed, small seed and cut seed, etc.


### 3.4 Body

The body of buffing machine supports and holds rotating perforated cylinders. This is stationary part of buffing machine, which is free on level ground. All the functional and revolving components of buffing machine are mounted on body.


### 3.5 Buffer Reel

The buffer reels separates the hydrolyzed linters from AHCl treated cotton seeds.  Buffer reels are perforated rotating cylinders.


### 3.6 Linter Collection and Conveying

Bottom hopper is provided for collection of linter. These linters are dropped on the screw fixed at the opening of the hopper and screw transfer the linters to bagging unit or trolleys are provided which collects the lint and then the trolleys are taken to the bagging unit.  Conveying can be done pneumatically or by screw conveyor.

## 6 CONSTRUCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS

The MS sheets, channels and angles used in the construction of buffing machine with air jet and nylon brush shall vary from 1.6 mm, 3 mm, 5 mm and 8 mm and angles 25 mm × 25 mm, 35 mm × 35 mm and 50 mm × 50 mm depending on the capacity of the machine. All the parts of machine should be Fibre Reinforced Plastic (FRP) lined 2 mm thick. Nylon brush and air jet system should be provided to clean the perforations.


## 7 OTHER REQUIREMENTS


7.1 The necessary bearing, chain sprocket and power drive shall be provided.


7.2 The necessary provision of greasing, oiling for bearings shall be provided.


7.3 The construction shall be such as to help easy repairs and maintenance.


7.4 The machine shall be provided with easy facilities for adjustments.


7.5 All the perforated screens must be Polytetrafluoroethylene coated with 40-50-micron thick coating.



### 7.6 No leakages of seeds through rotating reels.


## 8 WORKMANSHIP AND FINISH


8.1 The components of the machine shall be free from crack and other visual defects.


8.2 The welding and joints, if any, shall not be porous and shall be smooth.


8.3 The corrosion resistance coating shall be provided to the body with FRP lining 2-mm thick shall be provided. Coloured resin should be used for painting over Fiber Reinforced Plastic (FRP) lining.


8.4 The control and adjustment provided shall be easy and smooth in operation.


## 9 MARKING AND PACKING


### 9.1 Marking

Each buffing machine with air jet and nylon brush shall be marked with the following particulars:

- a) Manufacturer’s name and address; b) Model number; c) Power rating; d) Drive details; e) Batch or code number, if any; and f) Year of manufacture.

9.1.1 Each buffing machine with air jet and nylon brush may also be marked with the overall size and approximate weight of machine or this information may be given in the catalogue supplied by the manufacturer.


### 9.2 BIS Certification Marking

Each buffing machine with air jet and nylon brush may also be marked with the Standard Mark.

## ANNEX A


(Clause 5)

METHODS OF TEST FOR BUFFING MACHINE WITH  AIR JET AND NYLON BRUSH

A-1 PRELIMINARY             RUNNING             AND ADJUSTMENTS

A-1.1 The buffing machine with air jet and nylon brush shall be installed  on hard surface preferably levelled.

A-1.2  The buffing machine with air jet and nylon brush shall be preliminary run for about 30 min before commencing the test. All the adjustments shall be carried out in accordance with the recommendations of the manufacturers.

A-2 TEST AT NO LOAD

After preliminary running is over, the buffing machine with air jet and nylon brush shall be run at no load for 10 min at the specific speed.  During and after the no load run, the visual observation of the buffing machine  shall not show the following:

- a) Presence of any marked vibration during the operation.

- b) Presence of undue knocking sound or rattling sound. c) Any marked rise in the bearing temperature. d) Any marked unusual wear or slackness in any component.
A -3 TEST AT LOAD

The buffing machine with air jet and nylon brush shall be operated at its input capacity specified by the manufacturer incorporating best combination of adjustment for a period of 30 min. During the running period rpm of drum shall be noted. The observed speed shall not be less than one percent of the specified speed.

A-4 LONG RUN TEST

The buffing machine with air jet and nylon brush shall be operated for a minimum period of 20-h duration.  During and after the operation no breakdown or defects shall be developed in the buffing machine.
