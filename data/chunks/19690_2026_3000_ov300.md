FOREWORD

This Indian Standard was adopted by the Bureau of Indian Standards, after the draft finalized by the Media and Entertainment Services Sectional Committee had been approved by the Service Sector Division Council.

In the digital age, video games, whether played online or offline, have become a ubiquitous form of digital entertainment for people of all ages. As the nature of these games continues to evolve and diversify, it becomes crucial to provide a clear and consistent framework for age-rating and content description for the benefit of consumers. This enables consumers, particularly parents and guardians, to make informed decisions about the suitability of gaming content for different age groups.

This Indian standard specifies the age-rating labels and content descriptors based on the content of video games and digital games that are published and distributed publicly, including through online mechanisms. By establishing these standards, we aim to promote a safer and more transparent gaming environment, which protects younger audiences from potentially inappropriate content while balancing the creative freedom of game studios.

This standard has been prepared to provide consumers of video games with age-specific content rating information to determine the appropriateness of game content made available within the territory of India. This standard shall be applicable for game publishers and electronic distribution platforms. It provides a clear methodology for categorizing game content and assigning appropriate age ratings, enhancing the overall transparency and accountability within the gaming industry.

The composition of the committee responsible for the formulation of this standard is given in Annex C.

For the purpose of deciding whether a particular requirement of this standard is complied with, the final value, observed or calculated, expressing the result of a test or analysis, shall be rounded off in accordance with IS 2 : 2022 ‘Rules for rounding off numerical values (second revision)’. The number of significant places retained in the rounded off value should be same as that of the specified value in this standard.

## 3 TERMS AND DEFINITIONS

For the purposes of this standard, the definitions given in IS 19226 and the following shall apply:


3.1 Age-Rating Mark — A classification assigned to video or digital games that indicates the suitability of the content for specific age groups based on the presence of elements such as violence, language, sexual content, or other mature themes.


3.2 Content Descriptor — Terms that accompany and aid an age-rating mark under this standard in providing specific information about the type of content that led to the age-rating classification, which assists consumer in making informed decisions.


### 3.3 Age-Rating and Content Descriptor Label — Display of age-rating classification and content description categories as specified under this standard for video or digital games.


## 4  AGE-RATING MARK CATEGORIES

Video or digital games published, or distributed, shall carry the appropriate Age-Rating Mark, in uppercase letters, which may be self-assessed, as provided in Table 1, except in cases of limited display size, as specified under 6.2.5 where a modified label may be applicable.


## 5  CONTENT DESCRIPTORS CATEGORIES

Video or digital games published, or distributed, shall carry the appropriate content descriptors in lowercase letters separated by commas, which may be self-assessed according to content within the game as provided in Table 2, except under the condition of limited display-size, as specified under 6.2.5 where a modified label may be applicable.
