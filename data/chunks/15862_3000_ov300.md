## 3 TERMINOLOGY

For the purpose of this standard following

definitions shall apply.


### 3.1 HCl Gas Generation Unit

This unit generates HCl gas by heating HCl liquid

acid along with chemical reagent. The HCl gas

generation unit generates HCl gas from HCl liquid

acid 33 percent concentration. This unit holds HCl

gas and it cools down the temperature of HCl gas to

atmosphere temperature. This HCl gas is further used

for cotton seed delinting in delinting reactor.


### 3.2 M S Polytetrafluoroethylene Coated Vessel

This is the cylindrical container where the HCl acid

is heated with the polyelectrolytes, so that HCl

in the form of gas is released. The MS

Polytetrafluoroethylene coated vessel holds


33 percent concentrated HCl acid for heating along

with polyelectrolytes to generate HCl fumes from

the liquid acid.


### 3.3 Glass Condenser/Column

This is specially designed coil type glass condenser

to condense and collect the HCl vapours. The HCl

gas is released from acid and allowed to pass through

this coil type condenser where the traces of moisture

are condensed and dry HCl gas is sucked by the gas

pump.


### 3.4 HCl Gas Suction Pump

This pump creates negative pressure and sucks all

the gases released through condenser. The pump

also compresses the gas to push the gas under

pressure to the delinting reactor.


### 3.5 HCl Acid Pump

This pump is used to pump and discharge of HCl

acid to the vessel from acid tank having the capacity

of 1 000 l/h and the discharge head is about 6 m. The

pump should be cleaned every day after its use with

fresh.


### 3.6 HCl Gas Storage Tank

This is the cylindrical tank to hold the HCl gas and

then to supply the gas under pressure to the delinting

reactor.

## 6 CONSTRUCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS

The MS plates, sheets, channels and angles used in

the construction of HCl gas generation unit shall

vary depending on the capacity of the machine.

The plate and sheet thickness may vary from 3 mm,

5 mm, 8 mm, 10 mm and angles 25 mm × 25 mm,

35 mm × 35 mm, 50 mm × 50 mm and channel size

100 mm × 50 mm, 125 mm × 75 mm.

The MS vessel should be preferably fabricated from


10 mm plate with two valves.

The MS vessel must be coated with 1 000 micron

polytetrafluoroethylene inside and outside for

corrosion resistance.


## 7 OTHER REQUIREMENTS


7.1 The necessary power drive shall be provided.


7.2 The construction shall be such as to help easy

repairs and maintenance.


7.3 The machine shall be provided with facilities

for adjustments.


7.4 All the joints and connections should be air-

tight and there are no leakages.


7.5 The support stand should be Fibre Reinforced

Plastic(FRP) lined 2 mm thick.


### 7.6 The openings for pressure and temperature

gauges should be kept at the top of the vessel and

also opening for oil and HCl acid insertion.


## 8 WORKMANSHIP AND FINISH


8.1 The components of the machine shall be free

from crack and other visual defects.


8.2 The welding and joints, if any, shall not be

smooth and non-porous.


### 8.3 The corrosion preventing coating, that is, FRP

lining 2 mm thick shall be provided to the body and

also the Polytetrafluoroethylene coating of 100

micron shall be provided to gas storage tank.


8.4 The control and adjustment provided shall be

easy and smooth in operation


### 8.5 X-ray testing of vessel.

METHODS OF TEST FOR HCl GAS GENERATION UNIT

A-1

AND

PRELIMINARY

RUNNING

ADJUSTMENTS

A-1.1 The HCl gas generation unit shall be installed

on hard surface preferably levelled.

A-1.2 The HCl gas generation unit shall be

preliminary run for about 30 min before commencing

the test. All the adjustments shall be carried out in

accordance with the recommendations of the

manufacturers.

A-2 TESTATNOLOAD

After preliminary running is over, the HCl gas

generation unit shall be run at no load for 10 min at

the specific speed. During and after the no load run

the visual observation of the HCl gas generation unit

shall not show the following:

- a) Presence of any marked vibration during the
operation.

Presence of undue knocking sound or rattling

b)

sound.

Any marked unusual wear or slackness in any

component.

Any marked leakage.

d)

A-3 TESTATLOAD

The HCl gas generation unit shall be operated at its

input capacity specified by the manufacturer

incorporating best combination of adjustment for a

period of 30 min. The unit shall run continuously

without any breakdown or leakage.

A-4 LONGRUN TEST

The HCl gas generation unit shall be operated for a

minimum period of 20-h duration. During and after

the operation no breakdown, defects or leakage shall

develop in the HCl gas generation unit.
