FOREWORD

This Indian Standard was adopted by the Bureau of Indian Standards, after the draft finalized by the Media and Entertainment Services Sectional Committee had been approved by the Service Sector Division Council.

The domain of sound and music services has experienced significant growth driven by advancements in digital technologies, streaming platforms, and evolving modes of content creation and delivery. In light of this expansion, the establishment of a uniform and harmonized set of terminologies has become essential to ensure clarity, consistency, and effective communication among stakeholders. This vocabulary standard is formulated keeping in view the current, common accepted usage in the media and entertainment sector. These terminologies do not supplant or supplement statutory definitions, and are aimed at providing simplified terminologies for ease of business and are in consonance with statutory definitions.

This vocabulary standard has been formulated with the objective of providing a list of terms and definitions relevant to sound and music services. The terms included cover a wide range of concepts and technologies associated with sound recording, reproduction, transmission, streaming, acoustic environments, performance, and user experience. This vocabulary standard is intended to serve as a foundational reference for developers, content creators, copyright owners, service providers, researchers, regulators, and consumers engaged in this field.

By adopting a common language, this vocabulary standard seeks to promote interoperability, enhance collaboration, and support innovation in sound and music services. The terminology will also aid in technical documentation, educational resources, regulatory frameworks, and international harmonization efforts.

It is recognized that certain terms have been specifically defined in statutes or other official documents. Accordingly, the definitions provided herein are intended to be clarificatory only, keeping in mind Media and Entertainment Industry daily usage, and are not in conflict with or supplanting those established under existing laws. Further, this vocabulary is not intended to be exhaustive, and the terms included herein are provided solely for clarification and ease of understanding.

The composition of the Committee responsible for the formulation of this standard is given in  Annex A.
