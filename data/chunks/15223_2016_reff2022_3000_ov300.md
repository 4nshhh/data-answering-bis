## 1 SCOPE

This standard covers the requirement of interior fittings for M1 category of vehicles in respect of following:

- a) The interior parts of the passenger compartment other than the rear-view mirror or mirrors,
- b) The arrangement of the controls,
- c) The roof or opening roof, and
- d) The seat-back and the rear parts of seats.

## 3 TERMINOLOGY

For the purpose of this standard the definitions given in IS 14272 and the following definitions shall apply.


### 3.1 Approval of a Vehicle — Approval of a vehicle means the approval of a vehicle type with regard to its interior fittings.



### 3.2 Vehicle Type — Vehicle type with regard to the interior fittings of the passenger compartment means vehicles of category M1 which do not differ in such essential respects as,

- a) the arrangement of controls;
- b) the lines and constituent materials of the body work of the passenger compartment;
- c) the performance of the protective system, if the reference zone within the head impact zone determined according to Annex E (dynamic evaluation) is chosen by the vehicle manufacturer.
Vehicles that differ only in the performance of the protective system(s) belong to the same vehicle type if they offer an equal or better protection for the occupants compared with the system or vehicle submitted to the technical service responsible for conducting the approval tests.


### 3.3 Reference Zone (see Notes 1 and 2) — The head impact zone as defined in Annex A, or at the choice of manufacturer, according to Annex E except (see Notes 1 and 2) :

- a) The area bounded by the forward horizontal projection of a circle circumscribing the outer limits of the steering control, increased by a peripheral band 127 mm in width; this area is bounded below by the horizontal plane tangential to the lower edge of the steering control when the latter is in the position for driving straight ahead (see Note 2);
- b) The part of the surface of the instrument panel comprised between the edge of the area specified in (a) and the nearest inner side-wall of the vehicle; this part of the surface is bounded below by the horizontal plane tangential to the lower edge of the steering control; and
- c) The windscreen side pillars.
NOTES 1 The reference zone is outlined without rear view mirror. The energy-dissipation test is accomplished without the rear view mirror. The pendulum shall not impact the mirror mounting.




2 The exempted area behind the steering wheel as defined by these clauses is also valid for the head impact area of the front passengers. In the case of adjustable steering wheels the zone finally exempted is reduced to the common area of the exempted zones for each of the driving positions which the steering wheel may assume. In the case where it is possible to choose between various steering wheels the exempted zone is determined by the use of the least favorable steering wheel having the smallest diameter.
