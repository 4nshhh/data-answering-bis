## 3 TERMINOLOGY

For the purpose of this standard following definitions shall apply.


### 3.1 Ammonia Neutralizer Machine

The ammonia neutralizer machine neutralizes the cotton seed treated with Anhydrous HCl (AHCl) gas in delinting reactor.  This machine neutralizes cotton seed using ammonia gas in the proper proportion. The neutralization of cotton seed takes place in the rotating horizontal cylinders of this machine. The AHCl treated seed having pH 4 to 5 is raised to seven due to neutralization process in this machine.


### 3.2 Neutralized Seed

The neutralized seeds are cotton seed after treatment with ammonia gas in the ammonia neutralizer machine. The neutralized cotton seed has pH 7 and this seed is safe to use for sowing.


### 3.3 Weighing Mechanism

The weighing mechanism in the ammonia neutralizer machines weighs cotton seeds and then the seeds are dropped in the rotating horizontal cylinder. The weighing mechanism helps to weigh exact quantity of seed to treat with proportionate ammonia gas quantity to neutralize the cotton seed.  The weighing is done with electronic load cell mechanism and weight is displayed by LED digital display.


### 3.4 Body

The body of ammonia neutralizer machine is a stationary part of machine which supports and holds rotating inner drum axially. The body of machine is fixed on the level and preferably hard surface.


### 3.5 Gas Flow Meter

Ammonia gas flow meter with float measures ammonia gas volumetrically. The gas flow meter controls the flow of ammonia gas in order to proportionately treat cotton seed with ammonia gas for its neutralization.

## 7 OTHER REQUIREMENTS


7.1 The necessary bearing, chain sprocket and power drive shall be provided.

7.3 The construction shall be such as to help easy repairs and maintenance.

7.5 There should be no crushing of seed and no leakages through rotary cylinder.

## ANNEX A


(Clause 5)

METHODS OF TEST FOR AMMONIA COTTON SEED NEUTRALIZER

MACHINE WITH AMMONIA VAPORIZER

A-1 PRELIMINARY     RUNNING     AND ADJUSTMENTS

A-1.1 The ammonia cotton seed neutralizer machine with ammonia vaporizer shall be installed on hard surface preferably levelled.

A-1.2 The ammonia cotton seed neutralizer machine with ammonia vaporizer shall be preliminary run for about 30 min before commencing the test.  All the adjustments shall be carried out in accordance with the recommendations of the manufacturers.

A-2 TEST AT NO LOAD

After preliminary running is over, the ammonia cotton seed neutralizer machine with ammonia vaporizer shall be run at no load for 10 min at the specific speed. During and after the no load run, the visual observations of the ammonia neutralizer machine shall not show the following:

- a) Presence of any marked vibration during the operation. b) Presence of undue knocking sound or rattling sound.

- c) Any marked rise in the bearing temperature.
- d) Any marked unusual wear or slackness in any component.
A-3 TEST AT LOAD

The ammonia cotton seed neutralizer machine with ammonia vaporizer shall be operated at its input capacity specified by the manufacturer incorporating best combination of adjustment for a period of 30 min. The machine shall run continuously without any breakdown. During the running period rpm of cylinder shall be noted. The observed speed shall not be less than one percent of the specified speed.

A-4 LONG RUN TEST

The ammonia cotton seed neutralizer machine with ammonia vaporizer shall be operated for a minimum period of 20-h duration. During and after the operation no breakdown or defects shall be developed in the ammonia cotton.
