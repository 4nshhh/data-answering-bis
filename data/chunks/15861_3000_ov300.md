## 3 TERMINOLOGY

For the purpose of this standard following definitions shall apply.



### 3.1 Pollution Control System

The pollution control system is to control the pollution caused by Anhydrous HCl ( AHCl ) and ammonia gas and linters powder in the delinting plant. This system collects the gases released and then it is passed on to the scrubber to dilute these gases. This avoids pollution of atmosphere due to gases generated within the plant. The pollution control system helps to maintain the environment within the plant cleaned and thus the pollution norms can be followed.


### 3.2 Body

This is the stationary frame for supporting and holding the various components of pollution control system like hoods, ducting, cyclone, scrubbers, chimney and many other accessories. All the other components like blower fan, scrubber motor, scrubber pipeline are mounted on the body of pollution control system.

## 5 PERFORMANCE REQUIREMENTS

When tested with the methods given in Annex A, the machine shall pass the test.


## 6 CONSTRUCTION REQUIREMENTS

The MS sheets and angles used in the construction of pollution control system with scrubber, cyclone, chimney, hoods and ducting shall be of relevant Indian Standard. The thickness of MS sheet may vary from 3 mm to 5 mm and angles for support 25 mm × 25 mm, 35 mm × 35 mm and 50 mm × 50 mm. All ductings, fan,  chimney, scrubber, hoods have to be lined with 2 mm Fibre Reinforced Plastic  (FRP) lining for corrosion resistance.


## 7 OTHER REQUIREMENTS


7.1 The construction shall be such as to help easy repairs and maintenance.


7.2 The machine shall be provided with  facilities for adjustments.


7.3 Rubber gaskets should be used at all joints to avoid leakages.


7.4 Water pump, nozzle and perforated pipes are to be used in wet scrubber for fine spray of lime water.


## 8 WORKMANSHIP AND FINISH


8.1 The components of the system shall be free from crack and other visual defects.


8.2 The welding and joints, if any shall be smooth and non-porous.


8.3 The corrosion preventing coating shall be provided to the body with FRP lining 2  mm thick.


8.4 The control and adjustment provided shall be easy and smooth in operation.

## ANNEX A


(Clause 5)

METHODS OF TEST FOR POLLUTION CONTROL SYSTEM WITH

SCRUBBER, CYCLONE, CHIMNEY, HOODS AND DUCTING

A-1 PRELIMINARY RUNNING AND ADJUSTMENTS

A-1.1 The  pollution control system with scrubber, cyclone, chimney, hoods and ducting shall be installed on hard surface preferably levelled.

A-1.2 The pollution control system  with scrubber, cyclone, chimney, hoods and ducting shall be preliminary run  at the specified speed for about 30 min before commencing the test.  All the adjustments shall be carried out in accordance with the recommendations of the manufacturers.

A-2 TEST AT NO LOAD

After preliminary running is over, the pollution control system with scrubber, cyclone, chimney, hoods and ducting shall be run at no load for 10 min at the specific speed.  During and after the no load run, the visual observation of  the pollution control system shall not show the following:

- a)   Presence of any marked vibration during the
operation.


- b) Presence of undue knocking sound or rattling sound. c) Any marked unusual wear or slackness in any component.
A-3 TEST AT LOAD

The pollution control system with scrubber, cyclone, chimney, hoods and ducting shall be operated at its input capacity specified by the manufacturer incorporating best combination of adjustment for a period of 30 min. The system shall run continuously without any breakdown. During the running period rpm of drum shall be noted. The observed speed shall not be less than one percent of the specified speed.

A-4 LONG RUN TEST

The pollution control system with scrubber, cyclone, chimney, hoods and ducting shall be operated for a minimum period of 20-h duration. During  and after the  operation  no breakdown or defects shall be developed in the pollution control system.
