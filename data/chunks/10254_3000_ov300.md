2. TERMINOLOGY


2.0 For the purpose of this standard, the following definitions shall

apply.


### 2.1 Angle of Cut -- The angle formed by the line joining the point of

share and wings of share in a horizontal plane.


### 2.2 Angle of Penetration -- The angle of inclination of the share

( uppermost line ) in the direction of travel when the share is fitted in its

working position.

### 2.3 Cutting Edge -- The edge(s) of the share which cut the soil.


### 2.4 Point of Share - The leading end of the cutting edge which

affects the penetration of the share into soil. This may be integral or

detachable.


### 2.5 Share --- The V-shaped or wedge-shaped part of the ridger which

penetrates into the soil and makes the cut below the surface.


### 2.6 Size of Share -- The horizontal distance between the wings of a

share.


### 2.7 Wing of Share -- The rear end of the cutting edge of the share.
