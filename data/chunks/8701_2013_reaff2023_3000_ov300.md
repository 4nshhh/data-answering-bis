FOREWORD

This Indian Standard (Second Revision) was adopted by the Bureau of Indian Standards, after the draft finalized by the Pesticides and Pesticides Residues Analysis Sectional Committee had been approved by the Food and Agriculture Division Council.

Temephos, technical is used in the preparation of insecticidal formulations for agricultural use.

Temephos is the accepted common name by the International Organization for Standardization (ISO) for the O, O, O', O' — tetramethyl O, O' — thiodi-p-phenylene bis (phosphorothioate).

The empirical formula, structural formula and the molecular mass of temephos are indicated below:

Empirical Formula Structural Formula Molecular Mass

C16H20O6P2S3 466.5

In the formulation of this standard, due consideration has been given to the provisions of the Insecticides Act, 1968 and the Rules framed thereunder. However, this standard is subject to the restrictions imposed under the Insecticides Act and Rules, wherever applicable.

This standard was first published in 1978 and revised in 1996 to incorporate HPLC method in addition to spectrophotometric method. This revision has modified the HPLC method. The spectrophotometric method has been deleted.

For the purpose of deciding whether a particular requirement of this standard is complied with, the final value, observed or calculated, expressing the result of a test or analysis, shall be rounded off in accordance with IS 2 : 1960 ‘Rules for rounding off numerical values (revised)’. The number of significant places retained in the rounded off value should be the same as that of the specified value in this standard.

## 3 REQUIREMENTS


### 3.1 Description

Temephos, technical shall be in the form of pale yellow to brown viscous liquid, free from extraneous impurities or added modifying agents.


3.2 The material shall also comply with the requirements specified in Table 1.


## 4 PACKING

The material shall be packed in HDPE containers. The containers shall be heat sealed with HDPE plugs and finally closed with HDPE screw caps. It shall also meet the general requirements given in IS 8190 (Part 2).

## ANNEX A [Table 1, Sl No. (i)]

DETERMINATION OF TEMEPHOS CONTENT

A-1 PRINCIPLE

The temephos content is determined by high performance liquid chromatography (HPLC) equipped with UV-VIS detector using internal standard technique.

A-2 APPARATUS

A-2.1 A high performance liquid chromatography system equipped with ultraviolet detector, and a printer plotter-cum-integrator or PC based data system is used for this determination. The suggested operative parameters are as follows, but can be changed if necessary, provided standardization is done.

Column : Spherical silica, 150 mm

length, 4.6 mm internal diameter stainless steel, 5 µm Mobile phase : n-Hexane: ethyl acetate

(95: 5, v/v) Solvent flow rate : 1.0 ml/ min Injection volume : 10 µl Detector : Ultraviolet detector (274 nm)

A-2.2 Analytical Balance

A-2.3 Microlitre Syringe

A-2.4 Standard Glassware

A-3 REAGENTS

A-3.1 n-Hexane — AR grade or equivalent

A-3.2 Ethyl Acetate — AR grade or equivalent

A-3.3 p-nitrophenyl p-nitrobenzoate (Internal Standard), of known purity

A-3.4 Temephos Reference Standard, of known purity


A-4 PROCEDURE

A-4.1 Preparation of Internal Standard Solution

Weigh 0.121 4 g of p-nitrophenyl p-nitrobenzoate into 50 ml volumetric flask and make up the volume up to the mark with mobile phase. Shake well to homogenize.

A-4.2 Preparation of Reference Standard Solution

Weigh 0.033 3 g of reference standard temephos of known purity in 50 ml volumetric flask. Add 5 ml internal standard solution (see A-4.1) and make up the volume to the mark with mobile phase.

A-4.3 Preparation of Sample Solution

Weigh a quantity of sample equivalent to contain 0.033 3 g of temephos into 50 ml volumetric flask and proceed as described in A-4.2.

A-5 ESTIMATION

Inject 10 µl of the reference standard solution into the high performance liquid chromatograph (HPLC) followed by the sample solution with the help of microlitre syringe. Measure the peak areas of standard and sample solution. Compute the percentage of the temephos content in the sample.
