FOREWORD

This Indian Standard was adopted by the Bureau of Indian Standards, after the draft finalized by the Travel, Tourism, and Hospitality Related Services Sectional Committee had been approved by the Service Sector Division Council.

Exhibitions play an important role in boosting economic growth, encouraging innovation, and supporting product development across various industries. It also plays a significant role to promote trade, promoting innovation, supporting industry collaboration, and enhancing consumer engagement. As the scale and complexity of exhibitions continue to grow, there is an increasing need for a structured, consistent, and safety oriented approach to their planning and execution. Additionally, exhibitions also contribute to regional economic development by promoting tourism, hospitality, logistics, and allied service sectors.

This standard has been developed to provide comprehensive requirements and recommendations for organizing exhibitions. It covers key aspects, including venue selection, infrastructure planning, operational coordination, safety and security management, emergency preparedness, and post-event evaluation and site clearance. By establishing clear expectations and uniform processes, the standard aims to enhance the quality, reliability, and overall experience for exhibitors, visitors, stakeholders, and supporting agencies.

The objective of this standard is to support exhibition organizers, exhibitors, and service providers in adopting a systematic approach that will contribute to improving operational efficiency, enhancing risk management, and the promotion of best practices across the exhibition sector.

The composition of the Committee responsible for the formulation of this standard is given in Annex D.

For the purpose of deciding whether a particular requirement of this standard is complied with the final value, observed or calculated, expressing the result of a test or analysis shall be rounded off in accordance with IS 2 : 2022 ‘Rules for rounding off numerical values (second revision)’.
