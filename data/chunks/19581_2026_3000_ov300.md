FOREWORD

This Indian Standard was adopted by the Bureau of Indian Standards, after the draft finalized by the Travel, Tourism, and Hospitality Related Services Sectional Committee has been approved by the Service Sector Division Council.

Beaches are important natural assets that provide recreation, support tourism, and help coastal communities earn their livelihood. Beaches also protect marine ecosystems and biodiversity. With growing tourism and environmental awareness, it is essential to manage beaches in a manner that is environmentally friendly, socially inclusive, and economically beneficial.

Sustainable beach management requires a well-planned and responsible approach to their operation and maintenance. This standard has been developed to provide comprehensive framework for operation and management of beaches, with a focus on environmental protection, visitor safety and security, infrastructure, services and long-term ecological health.

The standard outlines key requirements for sustainable beach management, including providing environmental information, fostering stakeholder education and awareness, maintaining high standards of water quality, and promoting environmentally responsible practices. It also highlights the importance of safety, security, ecological conservation, and maintaining essential beach infrastructure and services.

By integrating these key elements, the standard aims to help authorities, operators and stakeholders in maintaining beaches that are safe, well managed, and environmentally responsible. It serves as a useful framework to improve beach quality, sustainability and user experience of beaches, ensuring these natural resources are protected for current and future generations.

This standard has been developed in alignment with national policies, environmental laws such as the Environmental Protection Act, Environmental Protection Rules 1986, Solid Waste Management Rules, 2016, and Coastal Regulation Zone (CRZ) notifications, and insights from experts and practitioners in beach management and sustainable tourism.

## 4 STATUTORY AND REGULATORY REQUIREMENTS


4.1 A Beach Management Committee (BMC) shall be constituted by appropriate authority for the purpose of sustainable beach operations along with its defined tenure and term of reference.


4.2 The BMC shall comprise relevant stakeholders, as appropriate, to ensure effective planning, implementation, and monitoring of beach related activities in line with the principles of sustainable beach operations, safety, and community participation. The indicative composition of the BMC is given in Annex C. The Committee should be reconstituted by the authority at prescribed period.


### 4.3  BMC Responsibilities

The BMC shall be responsible for the development, implementation, monitoring, and review of applicable plans as well as the overall operations, safety and compliance in accordance with the

provisions of this standard. The responsibilities of the BMC shall include, but not be limited to, the following:

- a) Ensuring compliance with all applicable
statutory and regulatory requirements;

- b) Securing necessary permissions from concerned authorities, and adhering to any government regulations issued from time to time;
- c) Approving guidelines for the operation, monitoring, and management of the beach;
- d) Disseminating all relevant information pertaining to the beach, including legal and other applicable requirements to stakeholders;
- e) Developing and implementing a code of conduct for beach use;
- f) Establishing, implementing and reviewing an emergency evacuation plan;
- g) Formulating, implementing, monitoring and reviewing guidelines for disaster management and beach restoration;
- h) Establishing, maintaining, implementing and reviewing a traffic management plan for the operation of eco-friendly shuttle services;
- j) Establishing, maintaining, implementing and reviewing an environmental management plan (EMP) to ensure the protection, conservation, and sustainable use of coastal and marine ecosystems;
- k) Conducting at least one meeting every quarter to review beach operations, with a minimum quorum of five members; and
- m) Conducting the Beach Suitability Assessment Study (BSAS) prior to the development of a sustainable beach as specified in 6.4.1.

ENVIRONMENTAL MANAGEMENT PLAN (EMP)

J-1 The environmental management plan shall be prepared to include criteria, but not be limited to, the following:

- a) Conservation and protection measures to safeguard flora and fauna, including turtle nesting and fish spawning areas;
- b) Conservation plan for migratory species, critically endangered species or other protected species; and
- c) Conservation plan for habitats like mangroves, sand dunes, rocky, or marshy
habitats etc, to ensure that such habitats are not disturbed or cleared;

J-2 While developing beach near a marine protected area or a wildlife sanctuary, the EMP shall be developed in consultation with the wildlife warden of the protected area; and

J-3 The consultation of domain area experts shall be taken for the development of EMP in case of areas identified as Ecologically Sensitive Areas (ESAs).
